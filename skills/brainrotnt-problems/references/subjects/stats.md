# Stats

Topics: descriptive statistics, probability distributions, sampling, confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, regression.

- Say whether a standard deviation or variance is the sample or population one, and how many decimals you expect. Set a tolerance that accepts reasonable rounding (0.005 relative is typical).
- Compute with the Python standard library (`statistics`, `math`); third-party packages such as numpy may not be installed where the bridge runs.
- Concept questions (what a p-value means) are `mcq` with plausible distractors.
- The `stats` calculator tier offers mean, median, standard deviation and similar functions.
