# Physics

Topics: kinematics, forces, energy, momentum, circular motion, waves, electricity.

- State every constant you expect to be used (for example g = 9.8 m/s²) so the key is unambiguous.
- Give `answer.unit` (m, s, J, N...). A student may omit the unit, but a wrong unit is marked incorrect.
- Use a relative tolerance around 0.01 unless the numbers are exact.
- Templates suit most kinematics and energy problems.
